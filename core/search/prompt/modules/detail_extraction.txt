DETAIL EXTRACTION GUIDELINES:

APPROACH:
- Look for specific attributes, properties, and characteristics
- FOR EVENT QUESTIONS: Find the specific event first, then read its fragment
- Check fragment content for detailed descriptions and sequences
- Examine relationship descriptions for additional details
- For "after X" questions: Look for the X event and read subsequent fragments

SEARCH STRATEGY:
- Primary: EVENT nodes with fragment reading (these contain the actual details)
- Secondary: Direct attribute mentions in ENTITY nodes
- Avoid: Relying solely on CONTEXT/STATE nodes (they're just background info)


ANSWER COMPLETENESS:
- Provide the specific detail requested
- Include relevant context if it aids understanding
- Acknowledge when details are limited or unavailable

ANSWER STYLE REQUIREMENTS:
✅ BE CONCISE - Answer directly without repeating the question
✅ NO ELABORATION - Give only what was asked
✅ AVOID REPETITION - Don't echo question words

ANSWER STYLE: Simple details for simple questions, detailed explanations for complex analysis

GOOD ANSWER EXAMPLES:
- Simple detail: "What challenges does Sam face?" → "Sam faces challenges like maintaining motivation and making dietary changes. He addresses them by enrolling in cooking classes and seeking support from friends like Evan."
- Complex analysis: "What role does nature play in their well-being?" → "Nature and outdoor activities seem to be significant stress relievers and sources of joy for both Evan and Sam. These activities likely contribute positively to their mental well-being."

FRAGMENT UTILIZATION:
- Original conversation text often contains richer details
- Look for descriptive language and specific mentions
- Cross-reference with structured node information
