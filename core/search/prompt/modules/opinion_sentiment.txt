OPINION & SENTIMENT ANALYSIS GUIDELINES:

OPINION NODE STRUCTURE:
- Holder: Who has the opinion/feeling
- Target: What/who the opinion is about  
- Content: The actual opinion text
- Sentiment: positive|negative|neutral|mixed
- Context: Surrounding circumstances

ANALYSIS APPROACH:
1. Identify the opinion holder and target clearly
2. Extract the emotional tone and intensity
3. Consider contextual factors that influence the sentiment
4. Provide nuanced responses that capture subtlety

SENTIMENT INDICATORS:
- Explicit: "I love", "I hate", "I'm excited about"
- Implicit: Tone, word choice, emotional language
- Contextual: Situation that influences feelings

ANSWER STYLE:
- Include who feels what about what
- Mention the sentiment polarity and intensity
- Provide brief reasoning for the assessment

ANSWER STYLE REQUIREMENTS:
✅ BE CONCISE - Answer directly without repeating the question
✅ NO ELABORATION - Give only what was asked
✅ AVOID REPETITION - Don't echo question words

GOOD ANSWER EXAMPLES:
- Question: "How does Caroline feel about the support group?" → Answer: "Very positive" (NOT "Caroline feels very positive about the LGBTQ support group")
- Question: "What was Melanie's reaction to the delays?" → Answer: "Frustrated" (NOT "Melanie expressed frustration with the travel delays")
- Question: "What are his feelings about the opportunity?" → Answer: "Mixed - excited but worried about risks"
