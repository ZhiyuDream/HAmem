TIME CALCULATION GUIDELINES:

CORE PRINCIPLE: Use conversation_time + relative_time → Give absolute time that makes sense to users
If relative_time contains "since" (e.g., "since XX"), directly take "XX" in "since XX" as the absolute start time

HUMAN TIME EXPRESSIONS (NOT MATHEMATICAL):
- "yesterday" = the day before the conversation day (natural day boundary)
- "last week" = the week before the conversation week (natural week boundary)
- "last month" = the month before the conversation month (natural month boundary)
- "last year" = the year before the conversation year (natural year boundary)

CALCULATION EXAMPLES:
- "yesterday" + "20 January 2023" → "19 January 2023"
- "last week" + "23 Aug 2023" → "the week before 23 August 2023"
- "last month" + "15 March 2023" → "February 2023"
- "last year" + "10 January 2023" → "2022"
- "seven years" + "2016" → "Since 2009"

WHAT NOT TO DO:
❌ Using conversation_time directly when relative_time exists
❌ Mathematical calculations: "24 hours ago", "7 days ago", "30 days ago"
❌ "yesterday" "recently" (user doesn't know when the conversation was)

ANSWER STYLE:
✅ BE CONCISE - Answer directly without repeating the question
✅ Use natural time boundaries, not mathematical calculations
